Policies & Terms

Please review the applicable BB SERVICES policies before booking or receiving service.

Important: Sending a payment does not by itself confirm a booking. Customers are responsible for following the booking, payment, property-access, and other requirements that apply to their service.

1. Payment, Pricing & Booking Policy

Payment Information

BB SERVICES may maintain records of payments received for its services. Payment records may include the payment date, payment amount, BB Booking Number, payment method, and other transaction information reasonably necessary to identify, verify, process, and maintain the payment record.

When paying through Cash App or Venmo, customers must include “For [Customer Name]” and the applicable BB Booking Number in the payment note or description, as instructed by BB SERVICES.

Customers are responsible for providing accurate payment information so that BB SERVICES can properly identify and apply the payment to the correct booking.

BB SERVICES does not sell, rent, or intentionally provide customer information to third parties for the purpose of selling or marketing customer information.

If BB SERVICES begins using Square or another third-party payment processor, the payment processor may collect, receive, process, or maintain customer and transaction information as necessary to provide payment-processing services. Information handled by a third-party payment processor may also be subject to that processor's own privacy policy, terms, and security practices.

BB SERVICES will only provide or transmit customer and payment information to a third-party payment processor when reasonably necessary to process, verify, record, or manage a customer payment or otherwise operate the applicable payment service.

Payment Identification

Customers are responsible for providing all required payment information accurately and completely.

Payment information must contain the information requested by BB SERVICES so that the payment can be properly identified and matched to the customer's booking.

Missing, incorrect, incomplete, or inaccurate payment information may delay BB SERVICES' ability to identify or apply the payment to the correct booking.

Until BB SERVICES is able to properly identify and verify a payment, the payment may remain Unverified and the related invoice may remain outstanding. This does not prevent an approved service from being scheduled or dispatched.

BB SERVICES is not responsible for delays in payment identification or booking confirmation caused by incorrect, incomplete, or missing information provided by the customer.

Customers should retain their payment confirmation or transaction information until BB SERVICES has confirmed that the payment has been properly received and applied to the applicable booking.

Service Confirmation & Payment Timing

A booking is officially confirmed when BB SERVICES has reviewed the request, confirmed the service details and availability, and sent the customer an official appointment confirmation.

Payment is not required before BB performs the service. After the service has been completed, BB will issue an invoice showing the amount due and the available payment methods and instructions.

Customers are responsible for paying the invoice according to its stated due date and instructions. Payment records will be updated after BB receives and verifies payment.

Payment Disputes

If a customer believes a payment was submitted to BB SERVICES, but the payment does not appear in BB SERVICES' payment records, the customer may contact BB SERVICES regarding the payment.

BB SERVICES may request reasonable transaction information or documentation to assist in locating, identifying, and verifying the payment. This information may include a transaction confirmation, transaction identification number, payment date, payment amount, payment method, or other information reasonably necessary to investigate the payment.

Customers are responsible for providing accurate information and reasonable documentation when requested to assist with payment verification.

A customer's statement that a payment was submitted does not, by itself, establish that the payment was successfully received by BB SERVICES.

Until a payment can be properly located, received, and verified, the related invoice may remain outstanding. This does not cancel a completed service or prevent an approved service from being dispatched.

BB SERVICES will review available payment information and communicate the applicable determination to the customer when the review is completed.

Reversed, Failed, or Disputed Payments

A payment will only be considered a valid payment toward a booking when the payment has been successfully received, verified, and retained by BB SERVICES.

If a payment is declined, fails, is reversed, canceled, refunded by the payment provider, disputed, charged back, or otherwise removed or not successfully retained by BB SERVICES, the payment will no longer be considered a valid payment toward the applicable booking.

If a payment is no longer valid, the customer remains responsible for any valid outstanding invoice balance. BB SERVICES may contact the customer to resolve the balance or take reasonable collection steps permitted by law. Payment issues do not retroactively cancel completed work and do not create a pre-service dispatch restriction.

If a payment provider independently reverses, removes, or otherwise withdraws a payment that BB SERVICES previously received, BB SERVICES may notify the customer by email when applicable.

Customers may be required to resolve payment issues directly with the applicable payment provider when the issue results from the payment provider's processing, reversal, dispute, chargeback, or other action.

A customer initiating a payment dispute or chargeback does not automatically establish that the original payment was invalid or that the customer is entitled to a refund. BB SERVICES may review the applicable booking, payment records, transaction information, and other relevant information before determining the appropriate resolution.

Invoice Due Dates & Outstanding Balances

Invoices are issued after the applicable service has been completed. Customers must pay the amount shown on the invoice by the due date or according to the payment instructions provided by BB.

If an invoice remains unpaid after its due date, BB may contact the customer regarding the outstanding balance and may take reasonable collection or account-management steps permitted by applicable law. An unpaid invoice does not retroactively change a completed service into a canceled appointment.

Payment Verification & Disputes

BB may request transaction details to locate and verify a payment. A payment is marked received once BB has confirmed that the funds were successfully received and retained.

2. Service Quality, Complaints & Re-Service Policy

Service Complaints

Customers may submit a complaint regarding a completed BB service and may be required to provide photographs, videos, documentation, service information, or other relevant evidence.

Administrative Review

BB Admin will review the complaint and available evidence to determine whether the booked service was properly completed according to applicable BB service requirements and the agreed scope of the booking.

A complaint based solely on a customer's personal preference or disagreement with BB's established service standards, procedures, or method of performing the service does not automatically qualify for a refund, complimentary re-service, or other compensation.

Evidence

BB may consider photographs, videos, booking information, service notes, employee reports, customer documentation, and other relevant information. BB Admin determines the relevance and reliability of submitted evidence.

Service Deficiency vs. Customer Preference

A service will not be considered deficient solely because a customer would have preferred a different appearance, method, result, level of detail, or manner of performance when the service was completed according to BB standards and the agreed scope.

Complaint Review

Customers should use the designated BB complaint process and provide sufficient information and supporting evidence. BB may request additional information when necessary.

BB Admin's review may take up to fourteen (14) calendar days from the date the complaint and required supporting evidence are received. Customers should not assume that a complaint has been approved, denied, or resolved until BB provides an official decision.

Approved Re-Service

If BB Admin determines that the evidence sufficiently establishes that the booked service was not properly completed, BB may approve a complimentary re-service at no additional charge.

Re-Service Scheduling

An approved complimentary re-service will be scheduled within five (5) to ten (10) days, subject to BB availability and circumstances affecting scheduling. Customers may communicate scheduling preferences but may not require or select a specific re-service date.

Approved Refund

If BB Admin determines that a complaint and supporting evidence establish that the booked service was not properly completed, BB may approve a full refund of the amount paid for the affected booking. Approved refunds are issued from BB business funds and processed within five (5) to ten (10) days.

3. Service Address & Property Access Policy

Accurate Service Address

Customers must provide the complete and accurate address where the booked service is requested.

Property Access

The service location must be reasonably accessible to BB personnel at the scheduled time. Customers are responsible for ensuring that BB personnel can access the property when they arrive.

Locked Properties

BB personnel will not wait for a locked property, gate, door, building, or other access point to be opened after arrival. Required access must be available when BB personnel arrive.

If the property cannot be accessed, BB may determine that the service cannot be performed. Any refund resulting from an access issue will be determined according to applicable BB policies and the circumstances.

Gate Codes and HOA Access

Customers must provide required gate codes and other necessary access information in the designated Property Notes section when applicable, including for HOAs, gated communities, apartment communities, neighborhoods, or property-managed locations.

Property Notes

Customers should provide gate codes, entry instructions, building information, unit numbers, parking instructions, and other information reasonably necessary to locate and access the service property.

4. Customer Property & Personal Belongings Policy

Customer Belongings

Customers are responsible for securing valuable, fragile, sensitive, or personally important belongings before service begins.

Access to Personal Property

BB personnel may handle or move customer property only when reasonably necessary to perform the booked service and in accordance with BB procedures.

Unsecured Items

BB is not responsible for delays, limitations, or changes to the service resulting from customer belongings that prevent BB personnel from safely or properly performing the booked service.

5. Damage, Existing Conditions & Pre-Existing Issues Policy

Existing Conditions

Properties, surfaces, furniture, fixtures, vehicles, equipment, landscaping, and other items being serviced may have existing damage, wear, stains, deterioration, defects, discoloration, or other pre-existing conditions.

Pre-Existing Conditions

BB is not responsible for conditions that existed before BB personnel began the service.

Documentation

BB may document existing conditions through photographs, videos, service notes, or other records when appropriate.

Customer Claims

If a customer believes BB caused damage during a service, the customer may submit a complaint with supporting evidence through the applicable BB complaint process.

6. Employee Safety & Workplace Conditions Policy

Safe Working Environment

Customers are responsible for notifying BB of known conditions at the service location that could reasonably affect employee safety or service performance.

Hazardous Conditions

BB personnel may refuse to enter, pause, modify, or discontinue a service when conditions present a safety concern, dangerous animal, hazardous material, unsafe property condition, or other circumstance that may reasonably place personnel at risk.

Unsafe Service Location

If BB determines that a location cannot be safely serviced, personnel may leave the property. Any refund or other resolution will be determined according to applicable BB policies and the circumstances.

7. Service Address Permission & Access Authorization Policy

Service Authorization

Customers must provide accurate information regarding whether the property owner, tenant, HOA, property manager, or other applicable party permits the requested service.

“Yes” Response

Selecting “Yes” represents that the customer believes the requested service is permitted and has a reasonable basis for providing that confirmation.

“Unsure” Response

Selecting “Unsure” may result in BB requesting additional information, confirmation, or administrative review. Selecting “Unsure” does not guarantee that the service will be performed.

Permission Problems

If BB determines before or during service that the requested service is not permitted, BB may decline, pause, or discontinue the service. Any refund or other compensation will be determined according to applicable BB policies and the circumstances.

8. Weather & Emergency Conditions Policy

Weather Conditions

BB may delay, reschedule, modify, or discontinue a service when weather conditions make the service unsafe, impractical, or unable to be properly completed.

Emergency Conditions

BB may take these actions because of emergencies, natural disasters, dangerous road conditions, property emergencies, utility problems, or other circumstances outside BB's reasonable control.

Scheduling

BB will determine the appropriate scheduling arrangement based on the circumstances, resources, and future availability. Customers may communicate scheduling preferences, but BB determines the applicable service date.

9. Employee Representation & Communication Policy

Employee Authority

BB personnel may communicate with customers regarding the services included in their booking. Employees are not automatically authorized to make binding decisions regarding refunds, cancellations, discounts, complimentary services, policy changes, booking changes, or other administrative matters.

Official Decisions

Customers should rely on official BB communications for administrative decisions. A statement made by an employee that has not been authorized or officially confirmed by BB does not automatically change a BB policy or booking.

Customer Requests

Submitting a request or concern does not guarantee approval. Administrative requests will be reviewed according to applicable BB policies and procedures.

10. Promotions, Discounts & Coupons Policy

Promotional Offers

BB may offer promotional pricing, discounts, coupons, or other special offers at its discretion. Each promotion may have its own terms, conditions, eligibility requirements, and expiration date.

Promotional Restrictions

Unless expressly stated otherwise, promotional offers may not be combined with other discounts, promotions, coupons, or special pricing.

No Retroactive Application

BB is not required to apply a promotion retroactively to a booking that was completed, paid for, or confirmed before the promotion became applicable.

Changes to Promotions

BB may modify, suspend, or discontinue a promotion subject to applicable promotional terms and obligations.
