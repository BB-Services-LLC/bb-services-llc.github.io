Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about BB SERVICES, available services, booking requests, scheduling, payments, and customer support.

What is BB SERVICES?

BB SERVICES is a multi-service company built around a simple idea: one company, multiple services, less hassle. We are building a convenient way for customers to access multiple types of services through one company.

How do I request a service?

Visit the Book a Service page, choose an available service, enter the requested details, and submit your service request. BB will review the information and follow up about availability and next steps.

Is my service request a confirmed appointment?

No. Submitting a request or receiving an estimate does not automatically confirm an appointment. BB must review the request, confirm the service details, date, and location, and complete the required confirmation process. Payment instructions will be provided with the invoice after the service has been completed.

Are all services available in every location?

Service availability may vary depending on the type of service requested, the service address, scheduling, and operational requirements. BB reviews each request and will let you know whether the requested service can be accommodated.

How does BB confirm an appointment?

After reviewing your request, BB will confirm whether the service can be provided and establish the applicable service details, date, and location. Your appointment is not confirmed until BB completes its required confirmation process. Payment is not required to confirm or schedule the service. An invoice will be issued after the service is completed.

What areas will BB serve?

BB is currently focused on Boston and communities throughout Massachusetts. Service availability may vary depending on the service requested, location, and scheduling.

Can I request a service that is not currently listed?

Yes. If there is a service you would like BB to offer, you can let us know. Customer feedback and service interest can help us understand what additional services may be valuable to the community.

If you have a custom service request, you can contact BB SERVICES directly by phone or email and tell us where you need the service and when you would like it done. We will review your request and determine whether we can accommodate it.

How do I book a service?

Use Book a Service to review available service categories and submit a booking request. A booking is not officially confirmed until BB completes its applicable confirmation process.

When is my service date confirmed?

BB SERVICES reviews each request and confirms the applicable service details, date, and location. Your appointment is confirmed when BB sends you an official confirmation.

Payment is not required before the service is performed. After the service is completed, BB will provide an invoice with the amount due and payment instructions.

When do I pay for my service?

Payment is due after the requested service has been completed. BB will issue an invoice showing the amount due, accepted payment methods, and payment due date or instructions. Please follow the details on your invoice.

What Should I Include in My Payment?

BB SERVICES currently accepts payment through Cash App, Venmo, and check. When submitting payment through Cash App or Venmo, customers must include their Job #/ID and the name used at the time of booking in the payment note or description. This information helps BB correctly identify and apply the payment to the appropriate booking.

If paying by check, please contact BB SERVICES for instructions. Payment should be submitted after the service is completed, according to the invoice and instructions provided by BB.

What if I have a complaint about a completed service?

Customers may submit a complaint through the designated BB complaint process. BB may request photographs, videos, documentation, or other evidence and will review the available information before making a decision.

Can I receive a free re-service?

A complimentary re-service may be approved when BB Admin determines that the evidence sufficiently establishes that the booked service was not properly completed. A customer preference or disagreement with BB's established service method does not by itself qualify.

What if BB cannot access my property?

The service address must be accessible when BB personnel arrive. Customers are responsible for providing required gate codes, building information, unit numbers, parking instructions, and other access information during booking.

What if weather or an emergency affects my service?

BB may delay, reschedule, modify, or discontinue a service when weather, emergencies, road conditions, utility issues, property emergencies, or other circumstances make service unsafe or impractical.

Can an employee approve a refund or change a policy?

BB personnel may assist customers with service-related questions, but employees are not automatically authorized to make binding decisions about refunds, cancellations, discounts, complimentary services, or policy changes. Customers should rely on official BB communications for administrative decisions.

How do I contact BB SERVICES?

Use the Contact BB SERVICES page for the current support phone number, temporary support email, service area, and contact-hour information.
