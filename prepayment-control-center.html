Post-Service Billing Workflow
1. Customer books
Booking creates or links the customer/job and invoice.
2. BB performs the scheduled service
The employee records completion and any required job notes.
3. BB issues invoice
The customer receives the invoice and payment instructions after service.
4. Customer pays
BB records and verifies the payment, then updates the invoice balance.
Company Payment Rule
SERVICE FIRST · PAYMENT AFTER

An unpaid invoice does not prevent an approved job from being scheduled, assigned, dispatched, or completed. Invoice collection is handled after service.

INVOICE PAID = BALANCE CLEARED
